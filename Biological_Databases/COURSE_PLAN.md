# Biological Databases & Information Systems — Course Plan

> **Programme:** Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech.) in Bioinformatics
> **Course Code:** CBBI-508 · **Semester:** Semester V
> **Credits:** 4 (L-T-P 2-0-2)
> **Academic Inspiration & Syllabus Source:** Department of Computational Biology & Bioinformatics, Jacob Institute of Biotechnology & Bioengineering (JSBB), [Sam Higginbottom University of Agriculture, Technology and Sciences (SHUATS)](https://shuats.edu.in/), Prayagraj, UP, India.

---

## 1. Course Overview
Database design principles, relational schemas (SQL), indexing biological sequence and structural data, graph databases (Neo4j), RESTful APIs, and cloud multi-omics data integration.

---

## 2. Official University Syllabus Excerpt
- **B.Tech. outline:** 4 credits, L-T-P 2-0-2
- **Syllabus PDF:** credit hours 4(2-0-4)

**Theory**

- **Introductions to Biological Databases:** Introduction- types, Biological databases, Information Retrieval from Biological Databases, Data management of biological information; Concepts, Data management concepts, Data management techniques, Challenges arising from biological data, Conclusions
- **Protein databases:** Primary structure databases, Functional protein Databases, Composite sequence database, Secondary structure database, Structural classification database.
- **Analyzing DNA, RNA and protein sequences in databases:** Genbank: organisms in Genbank, genomic DNA databases, cDNA databases corresponding to expressed genes, Expressed sequence tags, Sequence tagged sites, Genome survey sequences, High throughput genomic sequence
- **NCBI:** introduction, Pubmed, Entrez, Blast, OMIM, Books, Taxonomy, Structure, Locus link etc.
- **Database Similarity Searching:** Database Searching- introduction, Heuristic Database Searching, BLAST, FASTA, Comparison of FASTA and BLAST, Database Searching with the Smith–Waterman Method.

**Practicals**

1. Introduction to NCBI.
2. Using Entrez to search Literature Databases.
3. Sequence alignment using BLAST (Basic Local Alignment Search Tool).
4. Sequence alignment using FASTA.
5. Multiple sequence alignment using ClustalW.
6. Introduction to the structure database PDB.
7. Sequence analysis using EMBOSS.
8. Exploring KEGG (Kyoto Encyclopedia of Genes and Genomes).
