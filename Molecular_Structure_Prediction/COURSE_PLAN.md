## 1. Course Overview
Homology modeling, fold recognition and threading, ab initio structure prediction, AlphaFold / ESMFold deep learning architectures, Ramachandran plot validation, and PyMOL/VMD visualization.

---

## 2. Official University Syllabus Excerpt
- **B.Tech. outline:** 4 credits, L-T-P 2-0-2
- **Syllabus PDF:** credit hours 4(2-0-4)

**Theory**

- **Quantum mechanics and concepts in molecular modeling;** Introduction – coordinate systems – potential energy surfaces – molecular graphics – surfaces - computer hardware and software – introduction to quantum mechanics – postulates – Schrödinger wave equation – Born-Oppenheimer approximation.
- **Advanced ab initio methods and Molecular mechanics;** Force field Practical considerations when performing ab initio calculations – Density functional theory - Empirical force field models – Bond stretching – angle bending – torsional term – nonbonding interactions – thermodynamics properties using a forcefield.
- **Energy minimization;** Energy minimization statement of the problem - Derivative and non derivative energy minimization methods –simplex – sequential univariate method – first order minimization methods: steepest descent method – conjugate gradient method - second order minimization method: Newton-Raphson method.
- **Molecular Dynamics and Monte Carlo Simulation;** Introduction, Using simple Model,time steps,Multiple steps, Setting up MD – continuous potential in MD Simulation Examples,Monte Carlo simulation,Random number generation,Difference in MD & MC,Estimating errors in computer simulation.

**Practicals**

1. To retrieve and analyze a PDB file from the RCSB site.
2. To visualize the 3D structure of protein using a visualization tool.
3. To predict the secondary structure of a protein using the GOR algorithm.
4. To compute the various physical and chemical parameters for a given protein using the ProtParam tool.
5. To predict the 3D structure of a protein using the Swiss-Model server.
6. To perform the energy minimization of 3D structure of a protein using the GROMOS96 force field implemented in SPDB Viewer tool.
7. To perform the molecular dynamics simulation using the GROMACS software.
