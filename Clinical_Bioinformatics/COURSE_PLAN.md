# Clinical Bioinformatics & Precision Medicine — Course Plan

> **Programme:** Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech.) in Bioinformatics
> **Course Code:** CBBI-612 · **Semester:** Semester VII
> **Credits:** 4 (L-T-P 2-0-2)
> **Academic Inspiration & Syllabus Source:** Department of Computational Biology & Bioinformatics, Jacob Institute of Biotechnology & Bioengineering (JSBB), [Sam Higginbottom University of Agriculture, Technology and Sciences (SHUATS)](https://shuats.edu.in/), Prayagraj, UP, India.

---

## 1. Course Overview
Translational genomics: ACMG/AMP variant interpretation guidelines, somatic cancer mutation profiling, pharmacogenomics (CPIC guidelines), rare disease diagnostics, and clinical EHR data pipelines.

---

## 2. Official University Syllabus Excerpt
- **B.Tech. outline:** 4 credits, L-T-P 2-0-2
- **Syllabus PDF:** credit hours 4(2-0-4)

**Theory**

- Introduction to clinical bioinformatics, its releted branches, applications etc.
- In Silico Gene Dicscovery. Computer assisted reading of DNA Sequences. Utilizing HapMap and Tagging SNPs.
- Clinical uses of microarrays:in cancer research, in analysis if signaling pathways,in the study of viral gene expression etc.
- Evaluating DNA Sequence variants of unknown biological significance, protein comparative sequence analysis and computer modeling.
- Online resources for the molecular contextualization of disease and web based resources for clinical bioinformatics.

**Practicals**

1. Databases related to clinical bioinformatics
2. Sequence retrival and manipulation
3. SNP identification and analysis
4. Comparative sequence analysis
5. Familiarity with web resourses for clinical assistance
