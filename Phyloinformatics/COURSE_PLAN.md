## 1. Course Overview
Probabilistic phylogenetic inference: Maximum Likelihood (RAxML, IQ-TREE), Bayesian MCMC (MrBayes, BEAST), molecular clocks, substitution models (GTR, JTT), and phylogenomics.

---

## 2. Official University Syllabus Excerpt
- **B.Tech. outline:** 4 credits, L-T-P 2-1-1
- **Syllabus PDF:** credit hours 4(2-1-2)

**Theory**

- **Molecular basis of evolution;** Evolutionary tree of life, Mechanism of evolution, Structure and Functions of Genes, Mutational Changes of DNA sequences.
- **Evolutionary Change of Amino acid Sequences;** Amino acid Differences and proportion of different Amino acids, Poisson Correlation Gamma Distances, Bootstrap variances and Covariances, Amino acid substitution Matrix, Mutation rate and substitution rate.
- **Evolutionary change of DNA sequences;** Nucleotide differences Between sequences, Estimation of the number of nucleotide substitutions, Sequence alignment and evolutionary distances, Evolutionary significance of genetic polymorphism
- **Phylogenetic Trees;** Types of Phylogenetics trees, Topological differences, Tree building Methods
- **Distance based tree building methods;** UPGMA, Least Squares methods, Minimum evolution methods, Neighbor joining.
- **Character based tree building methods;** Strategies of searching for MP trees, Weighted Parsimony, Maximum Likelihood Methods, Computational procedure of ML methods, Protein likelihood methods, Parameter estimation for a given topology
- **Phylogenetic information analysis;** Phylogenetic information and the need for a phylogenetic database, The Tree of Life Web Project, Tree pattern matching in phylogenetic trees, Automatic search for orthologs or paralogs, BranchClust: A phylogenetic algorithm for selecting gene families, Phyutility: a phyloinformatics tool for trees, alignments and molecular data, Phylofinder- A search engine for tree databases
- **Statistical analysis and test of phylogenetic trees;** Topological error and test of topological differences, Interior branch test and Bootstrap analysis, Advantages and disadvantages of tree building methods

**Practicals**

1. To perform phylogenetic analysis based on selected proteins.
2. To get familiar with Phylogenetic Inference Package.
3. To perform Phylogenetic analysis using parsimony.
4. To perform Phylogenetic analysis using Maximum Liklihood.
