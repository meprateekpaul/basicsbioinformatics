> **Programme:** Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech.) in Bioinformatics
> **Course Code:** CBBI-614 · **Semester:** Semester VII
> **Credits:** 4 (L-T-P 2-0-2)
> **Academic Inspiration & Syllabus Source:** Department of Computational Biology & Bioinformatics, Jacob Institute of Biotechnology & Bioengineering (JSBB), [Sam Higginbottom University of Agriculture, Technology and Sciences (SHUATS)](https://shuats.edu.in/), Prayagraj, UP, India.

---

## 1. Course Overview
Structure-based and ligand-based drug design, pharmacophore modeling, quantitative structure-activity relationships (QSAR), virtual screening pipelines, lead optimization, and ADMET profiling.

---

## 2. Official University Syllabus Excerpt
- **B.Tech. outline:** 4 credits, L-T-P 2-0-2
- **Syllabus PDF:** credit hours 4(2-0-4)

**Theory**

- **What are drugs? Receptors:** types, theory of receptors, Mechanism of drug action, Pharmacodynamics, Pharmacokinetics- Absorption, Distribution, Metabolism, Excretion & Toxicity.
- Structure based drug design (SBDD), Target identification & validation, Lead finding & optimization, Lipinski's rule, De novo drug design, methods, Virtual screening, Databases and Tools used in SBDD.
- Ligand based drug design (LBDD), Quantitative Structure-Activity Relationships (QSAR)- Parameters, Descriptors, 2D and 3D QSAR, Statistical Techniques, Inverse QSAR, Software & databases for QSAR. Pharmacophore- Fundamentals of pharmacophore mapping, Identification of pharmacophoric groups. Approaches used in pharmacophore modeling.
- Software tools of pharmacophore modeling.
- Docking, Concepts, Scoring functions, Algorithms, Protein-ligand, Protein-protein & Protein- DNA docking. Software tools for Docking.

**Practicals**

1. To perform the homology modeling of a protein by using the MODELLER software.
2. To evaluate the 3D structure of a protein by using the different tools on the SAVS server.
3. To find the active site/cavity in a receptor by using the SURFACE RACER program.
4. To draw the chemical structures by using the ChemSketch/ChemDraw software.
5. To perform the de novo Drug Designing by using the LIGBUILDER software.
6. To perform the protein-ligand docking by using the HEX program.
7. To perform the protein-protein docking by using the AUTOCOCK software.
8. To perform the virtual screening of ligands by using the DOCK program
